Our Story

A cooperative built
on community & craft

Artisans in the Square opened in 2010 in historic Hingham Square with a simple idea: give local makers a beautiful space to sell their work alongside the very people who made it. When you visit, you're likely to be greeted by one of our artisans working behind the counter — meaning you can meet the creator of anything that catches your eye.

Every piece is original and handmade. We don't sell on consignment — each member is a working co-owner of the shop. New work arrives daily, and we happily welcome custom orders.

Meet the maker

Our artisans staff the shop themselves, so you can talk directly with the person who created the piece you love — and even place a custom order.

Something for every price point

From greeting cards and small gifts to statement pieces of blown glass and handthrown pottery — there's something beautiful at every budget.

Proudly local, deeply rooted

Our artisans live and work on Massachusetts' South Shore, and the shop has been a beloved part of Hingham Square for over fifteen years.

What we make

Crafts for every taste

Our cooperative spans over ten distinct mediums — from the ancient art of glassblowing to delicate fiber arts — each practiced to a high standard.

Blown Glass
Handblown drinkware and home décor inspired by the natural world, crafted in Cambridge studios.
Pottery
Wheel-thrown, functional pieces with beautiful surface decoration — microwave and food safe.
Artisan Jewelry
Handcrafted pieces from fine metals, gemstones, and unique materials found along the South Shore coast.
Cape Cod Baskets
Traditional Nantucket and Cape Cod-style baskets woven in the authentic New England tradition.
Fiber Arts
Woven textiles, needle felting, and chenille work — from tea towels and placemats to handcrafted clothing.
Soaps & Candles
Artisanal hand-poured soaps and candles, beautifully crafted and perfect for gifting.
Photography
Fine art photography of New England's landscapes, seascapes, and quiet moments, printed on archival paper.
Paper Goods
Handmade cards, journals, and paper gifts — thoughtfully crafted and ideal for every occasion.
